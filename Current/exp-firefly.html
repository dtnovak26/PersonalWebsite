Introduction

At Firefly I was challenged to do as much as I possibly could and I am proud to have learned a ton. Working as a manufacturing engineer I learned how to improve designs from an engineering standpoint, as well as from actually being hands on the floor. Most days started with the first ~8 hours doing engineering work, writing work instructions, adapting parts, and firefighting upstairs. Then, for the next 2-6 hours I would be on the floor working with technicians on my parts (or whatever needed assistance that day!) hands on to learn from the people actually doing the work. Because I was the only intern for the department in the sprint I got to work on all the major projects Firefly has. My projects can be split up into spacecraft, Alpha, Eclipse, and process improvement.
