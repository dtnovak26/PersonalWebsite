Semiconductor Manufacturing Intern

Internship Purdue STARS — Purdue University Summer 2024

Worked on the MIM (Metal-Insulator-Metal) Characterization subteam within Purdue's STARS program to research and improve photolithography masks used in fabricating MIM capacitors at the Birck Nanotechnology Center cleanroom. Presented findings at the Purdue Summer Research Convention and toured GlobalFoundries Fab 8 in Malta, NY.

  • Compiled results into a technical presentation delivered at the Purdue Summer Research Convention.
  • Toured and received a technical briefing at GlobalFoundries Fab 8, a leading-edge semiconductor manufacturing facility in Malta, NY.
