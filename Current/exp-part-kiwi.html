Deep Dive: What I Did

Introduction

KIWI is Purdue's autonomous aerial systems student team. Unlike the freestyle-focused side of drone work, KIWI is oriented around building fully autonomous vehicles that can navigate, detect, and complete mission objectives without human input.

My Role

I joined KIWI to push deeper into the autonomy and controls side of UAV work — something that connects directly to my interest in electromechanical integration and controls systems. My work has touched on mechanical sub-assembly, sensor integration, and interfacing with the software stack.

Looking Forward

Autonomy is one of the most exciting areas of engineering right now and KIWI gives me a front-row seat to apply that in a competitive, hands-on context. I'm looking forward to contributing more heavily to the sensing and controls pipelines.
